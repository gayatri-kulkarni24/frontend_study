shri vasudeva krishna

Krishna is a major Hindu god and a central figure in many Hindu texts, including the Bhagavad Gita.
He is the eighth incarnation of Vishnu, the Hindu supreme god, and is also worshipped as a supreme god in his own right.
Krishna is known as the god of compassion, protection, and love, and is also known as the Dark One.
He is often depicted in Indian art and mythology in many different ways, including as a child-god, divine lover, prankster,
hero, warrior, teacher, and philosopher
